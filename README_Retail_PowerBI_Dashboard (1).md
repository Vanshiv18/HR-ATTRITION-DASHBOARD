## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Retail Sales Analytics Dashboard** developed in Microsoft Power BI. It transforms retail transaction data into meaningful business insights related to sales performance, product categories, outlet performance, customer preferences, and key performance indicators.

The dashboard is designed to support data-driven decision-making through interactive visualizations, filtering, and business performance analysis.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Monitor important retail sales KPIs and business performance indicators.
- Analyze sales trends across product categories and outlet characteristics.
- Identify high-performing products, outlets, and customer preferences.
- Present actionable insights through interactive Power BI visualizations.
- Support business decisions related to product mix, outlet performance, and sales strategy.

## 📊 Dashboard Highlights

- **KPI Tracking:** Total sales, average sales, item count, and rating-related metrics.
- **Product Analysis:** Comparison of sales performance across product categories.
- **Outlet Analysis:** Evaluation of outlet size, location, and establishment-related patterns.
- **Customer Insights:** Analysis of customer preferences and product visibility.
- **Interactive Reporting:** Slicers, filters, and dynamic visual exploration.
